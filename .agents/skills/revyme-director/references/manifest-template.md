## 6. Page Structure & Spacing Rhythm

*Rule: Section padding-top $\ge 1.5\times$ padding-bottom. Gap between groups $\ge 2\times$ item gap.*

- **Header / Nav**: Fixed or sticky, height: 64–72px, backdrop blur or solid tonal surface.
- **Hero Section**:
  - Height: min 90vh on Desktop.
  - Layout: Asymmetric split (Headline & Lead left, Signature 3D element right) or centered cinematic lockup.
- **Section 2 (Value / Bento Grid)**:
  - Responsive 3-column / 2-column on Desktop, collapsing to single column on Mobile.
- **Section 3 (Showcase / Detail)**:
  - Generous negative space, large typography accents.
- **Footer**:
  - Clean semantic links, copyright, minimal brand mark.
